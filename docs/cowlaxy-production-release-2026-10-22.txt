WHAT IS AVAILABLE
Upload a supported Store item, choose Moola price or lifetime XP target, save a
draft, then publish. Publishing an XP item atomically creates its scoped discovery.
Characters, skins, planets, keys, gadgets, badges, themes and titles use the existing
supported digital-item inventory. This does not add arbitrary game mechanics or
physical merchandise fulfilment. XP is never spent. The app shows the target and
progress; eligible Store-only visits trigger server confirmation. Earned ownership
survives target edits and archival. Built-in key fragment: 100 XP; glow: 1,000 XP.
Preset Brumble/Klik/Lumi/Pip XP grants were removed; upload characters to configure
their acquisition. Existing earned metadata/artwork remains supported.
Only existing verified learner reward accounts receive these discoveries; no
legacy account migration or reward activation was performed by deployment.
